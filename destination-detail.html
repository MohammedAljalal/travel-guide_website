AlUla

The World's Masterpiece

Destination Overview

Journey through time in AlUla, an ancient oasis in a desert valley in the Madinah Region. It features spectacular monumental tombs, breathtaking sandstone canyons, and luxury resorts carved into nature.

Climate
Dry and warm; ideal visiting season from October to April.
Currency
Saudi Riyal (SAR). Cards widely accepted in resorts.
Language
Arabic is official, English is widely spoken in tourism.
Transport
Luxury private transfers, rental cars, and domestic flights to AlUla International Airport.
Travel Costs
Premium luxury pricing ($$$$)
